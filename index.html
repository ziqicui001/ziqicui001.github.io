Human-Centered Urban AI

Spatially explicit AI, multi-agent LLMs and multimodal human data — from EEG and eye-tracking to street view and mobility traces — for human-centered urban design.

Ziqi Cui

Ph.D. Student · The University of British Columbia

I'm a researcher working on the quantification and analysis of urban data. My goal is to integrate multi-source data and advanced modeling to uncover complex human–environment relationships and advance human-centered urban development.

Since September 2026, I have been a Ph.D. student at The University of British Columbia. Before that, I was a Research Fellow at the Laboratorio di Simulazione Urbana Fausto Curti (LABSIMURB), Politecnico di Milano, working with Prof. Barbara Ester Adele Piga on EU-funded projects (NextGenerationEU MED4PED and MUSA), and a Research Assistant at the Department of Computer Science, University of Milan. I hold an M.Sc. in Advanced Computation for Architecture & Design from IAAC (Barcelona), an M.Sc. in Architecture – Built Environment – Interiors from Politecnico di Milano (110/110), and a B.Eng. in Architecture from Shanxi University.

My recent research focuses on:

  • Human-subject experiments in VR and on-site, collecting multimodal data (EEG, ECG, EDA, eye-tracking, questionnaires, behavior) to uncover cognitive mechanisms of human–environment interaction.
  • Macro-level evidence from social media, reviews and street view imagery for generalizable insights into public perception.
  • Spatially explicit & explainable AI — graph neural networks, vision–language models and LLM-based multi-agent systems for perception and human mobility prediction.
  • Decision-support tools such as 3D modeling plugins and dashboards that turn analytical insights into practice.
