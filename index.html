Human-Centered GeoAI

Ziqi Cui

Ph.D. Student · The University of British Columbia

I'm a researcher working on the quantification and analysis of urban data. My goal is to integrate multi-source data and advanced modeling to uncover complex human–environment relationships and advance human-centered urban development.

Since September 2026, I have been a Ph.D. student at The University of British Columbia. Before that, I was a Research Fellow at the Laboratorio di Simulazione Urbana Fausto Curti (LABSIMURB), Politecnico di Milano, working with Prof. Barbara Ester Adele Piga on EU-funded projects (NextGenerationEU MED4PED and MUSA), and a Research Assistant at the Department of Computer Science, University of Milan. I hold an M.Sc. in Advanced Computation for Architecture & Design from IAAC – Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (Barcelona), an M.Sc. in Architecture – Built Environment – Interiors from Politecnico di Milano (110/110), and a B.Eng. in Architecture from Shanxi University.

My recent research focuses on:

  1. Human-Centered Geospatial Representation Learning. Developing multimodal geospatial representations that integrate physical environments with human perceptions, experiences, and activity-specific affordances.
  2. Multiscale Human–Environment Interaction. Investigating human activities through large-scale social and behavioral data and fine-grained psychophysiological measurements (EEG, ECG, eye-tracking, etc.).
  3. Human-Centered Urban Intelligence. Advancing AI-driven spatial reasoning, context-aware place retrieval, and activity-specific environmental assessment to support human-centered urban planning and decision-making.
